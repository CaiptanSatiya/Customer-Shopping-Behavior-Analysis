# Customer-Shopping-Behavior-Analysis

## Objective:
This project analyzes customer purchasing behavior to understand buying patterns, subscription adoption, product performance, and demographic trends. An interactive dashboard was built to help businesses make data-driven marketing and sales decisions.

## 📊 Key Metrics Overview

* Total Customers: 3,900
* Average Purchase Amount: $59.76
* Average Review Rating: 3.75
* Subscription Status:
* Subscribed: 27%
* Non-Subscribed: 73%

## 🧭 Analysis Highlights
### 🔹 Revenue & Sales by Category

* Clothing is the top-performing category in both revenue and sales volume
* Accessories contribute moderately, while Footwear and Outerwear show lower demand
* Indicates strong customer preference toward apparel products

### 🔹 Age Group Analysis

* Young Adults generate the highest revenue and sales volume
* Middle-aged customers closely follow, showing consistent purchasing behavior
* Senior and adult segments contribute steadily but at slightly lower levels

### 🔹 Subscription Insights

* A majority of customers are non-subscribers, indicating an opportunity to increase subscription adoption
* Subscription-based strategies could help improve repeat purchases and customer retention

### 🔹 Customer Preferences & Filters

#### Analysis supports filtering by:
* Gender
* Subscription status
* Product category
* Shipping type
* Enables detailed segmentation and targeted analysis

## 🧠 Business Value Delivered

* Identified high-performing customer segments and product categories
* Highlighted opportunities to improve subscription conversion
* Supported targeted marketing and inventory planning
* Delivered a clear, interactive view of customer behavior patterns
